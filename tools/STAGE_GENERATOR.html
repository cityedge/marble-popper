Target length controls the generated IN-to-OUT distance. Length tolerance defines the acceptable band around it. Minimum screen reach independently asks the route to span that percentage of both the usable width and height, so a shorter route can still travel widely across the board. The generator searches toward both goals and reports when either target is missed. Simplify remains a separate finishing pass and can shorten the exported final route below the generation target; use a low value when calibrating length. Crossing topology, clearance, edge bounds, and the family-specific base remain protected. Export StageSpec records the requested constraints and achieved measurements.
